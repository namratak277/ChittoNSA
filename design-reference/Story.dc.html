CHHITO! · THE GAME STORY

The Climb to the Ballot

Teams of climbers try to reach the summit of Everest. At each checkpoint they face weather, supplies, navigation and surprises, and every decision matters. The twist: the skills that get you up a mountain, preparation, reliable information and a backup plan, are the same ones that help you vote.
1 · BASE CAMP
Get prepared
Spend 10 supply points, then answer: what should you check before Election Day?
Packing gear = knowing your registration status and eligibility.
2 · ROUTE CHECKPOINT
Trust the right map
Three info cards: one official, one outdated, one unverified social post. Pick the one to trust and say why.
Studying the route = knowing where, when and how to vote.
3 · STORM ZONE
Plans change
A surprise event card. Two minutes to agree on a plan. Practical, informed answers win, not the fastest.
Checking the weather = watching for changes to locations, deadlines or rules.
4 · FINAL ASCENT
Put it together
One day left. Explain your plan, what you'd double-check, and what you'd do if something goes wrong.
A backup plan = knowing your other voting options.
5 · SUMMIT
Make your voting plan
Everyone fills in their own plan on their phone: when, how, what's required, where to check for updates.
Reaching the summit = making your voice heard.
