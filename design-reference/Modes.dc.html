Two ways to answer · two kinds of people

The host picks the mode in Host setup before Base Camp opens.
MODE 1 Buttons mode
Everyone answers every question. Tap the trail sign that matches the flag before the 20-second timer ends.
Scoring: a right answer earns 10 points for the team. Faster teams earn a small speed bonus, and every team keeps climbing.
Best for: big groups and warm-ups. Nobody sits out.
MODE 2 Buzzer mode
Solve a quick puzzle to buzz in: hang the five prayer flags in the right order. The order you finish in is the order you answer in.
Scoring: the 1st team to buzz gets 10 seconds to answer. Right = 10 points. Wrong = no penalty, and the next team gets a turn.
Best for: finals and smaller groups. Fast and dramatic.
Game maker · needs an account
Signs in on a laptop. Sees a work-style dashboard: a list of saved games, a question builder, mode and timer settings, and host controls (Start, Reveal, Next, Storm event, award team points). Runs the projector screen.
Player · just the game PIN
No account, no download. Opens the link or scans the QR code, types the PIN and a nickname, and picks a team by flag colour. Sees only big, simple game screens, plus their own camp and result.
